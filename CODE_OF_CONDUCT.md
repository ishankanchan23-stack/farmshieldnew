# Community guidelines

FarmShield AI is an educational project. Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information. Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and may limit participation where needed to keep the community safe.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Include enough context to investigate; do not post private or sensitive material in public issues.
